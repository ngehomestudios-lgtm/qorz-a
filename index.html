Qorz-a Studio

Qorz-a Studio is a private, single-user tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It edits gaming highlight clips into short vertical videos and publishes them to the Qorz-a channel's own accounts (YouTube, and optionally TikTok and Instagram).

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's accounts.
